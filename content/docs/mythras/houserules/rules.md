# House Rules and Rules Modifications
## Statistics
### Action Points
If you have 2 or less AP, you gain 1 bonus AP, however, this bonus AP can only be used on the Evade and Parry Reactive Actions.

### Luck Points
Instead of rerolling the d100 die, you can optionally swap the 10's and 1's place, giving you a more 'guaranteed' result.  
Example: Turning a 71 into a 17.

## Downtime
### Healing (TRIAL)
Accelerated. 
- Minor wounds always heal within one day, even when engaged in strenuous activity.
- Serious wounds heal at healing rate speed every two days. Engaging in strenuous activity reduces the healing rate by 1d3, with a minimum total healing rate of 1.
- Major wounds heal at healing rate speed every week. Engaging in streneous activity reduces the healing rate by 1d3, with a minimum total healing rate of 0.
