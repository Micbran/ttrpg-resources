# Intro
This is part of the talents system I want to graft onto Mythras. Basically just feats / spheres. Nothing super complicated. Also comes with alternate progression (GM / Milestone - I could do actual XP as well).

## Existing Content
The closest existing concept to this in Mythras is the boons from fighting styles. I don't want overlap on these -- I want them to maintain their uniqueness. So, I'm going to write down several of them. The other are gifts, which are from the Cults / Brotherhood section.

### Utility Talents
#### Skills (Standard)
##### Athletics
###### Armored Athlete
When using the athletics skill (running, sprinting, climbing and swimming), if you would subtract half your armor penalty, you instead subtract none. If you would subtract your full armor penalty, you instead subtract half (rounded up). If you would subtract twice your armor penalty, you instead subtract your armor penalty.

###### Skilled Leaper
You only fall prone from jumping when your jump exceeds your maximum distance (such as from falling some extra distance). Your maximum jumping distances become three times your height horizontally and three-fourths your height vertically, as long as you have a five meter run-up. Your maximum jumping distances for standing jumps becomes two times your height horizontally and one-half your height vertically.

###### Fleet-Footed
You add one meter to your base Movement when sprinting and half that when running over long distances for every 15% of Athletics, instead of every 25%.

##### Boating
###### Land Boat
You may use your Boating skill instead of your Drive skill, at one grade of difficulty harder.

##### Brawn
###### Hefty Toss
For every 10% of Brawn, add 1 to your STR for the purposes of determining the maximum range you can throw an object using the Athletics skill. If you succeed at a Hard Brawn roll, you may add your STR again to this calculation. You may always augment Athletics checks to throw with your Brawn. **Special** If you have Applied Force, you instead add one to your STR for every 7% of Brawn.

###### Applied Force
When using the Brawn skill in a contest of strength, you may ignore one increase in difficulty grade that arises as a result of having a smaller Damage Modifier than your opponent's. You add one to your lifting capacity for every 7% of Brawn instead.

##### Conceal
###### Tricky Concealment
When you at least succeed at a Conceal roll, attempts to locate the thing concealed suffer a penalty equal to 20% of your Conceal.

##### Customs
###### Careful Custom(er)
You can never fumble a Customs roll, instead receiving the results of an embarrassing, but harmless failure. If you are not under any pressure and the difficulty grade is not anymore difficult than standard, you may choose to not roll and instead receive a success.

###### Lucky Dodge

##### Stealth
###### Stealth Assistance
As long as you at least succeed at a Stealth roll, you may help hide one or more allies along with you, depending on whether you are stationary or moving. These allies gain the benefits of a success on Stealth as long as they did not fumble and are within one meter of you (and stay within one meter).
- If stationary, you may hide one ally plus one additional ally for every 15% of Stealth.
- If moving, you may hide one ally plus one additional ally for every 30% of Stealth.
**NOTE** This does not make it harder to find the character once they _are_ stealthed, just allows the characters to take Stealth more easily.

###### Tricky Stealth
As long as you at least succeed at a Stealth roll, attempts to locate you suffer a penalty equal to 20% of your Stealth.
